# Hero Design Intent

## Overall Atmosphere

The hero section should feel like an open stage.

The entire scene is warm, welcoming and spacious, built around a soft beige color palette.

Nothing should feel cramped, boxed in or visually heavy.

Soft lighting gently spreads from both sides of the hero, adding depth and atmosphere without distracting from the main content.

The hero should immediately draw attention toward the person standing at the center of the composition.

---

## Main Content

The name "Phạm Quốc Hưng" should appear prominently in the upper-left area of the hero.

It should feel confident, professional and easy to read.

Below the name, a short introduction presents personality, expertise and identity.

A clear call-to-action button invites visitors to explore projects and learn more.

The text area should remain visually separated from the character while still feeling connected to the overall composition.

---

## Character

The character is the primary focal point of the hero.

The character stands facing the visitor, creating a direct and personal connection.

Behind the character there should only be the beige background and subtle lighting effects.

The area around the head, shoulders and upper body must remain visually clean.

Nothing should appear behind the character that competes for attention or creates visual clutter.

The character should always remain clearly visible.

---

## Desk

A curved transparent desk appears near the bottom of the hero.

The character stands behind this desk, similar to standing behind a counter, reception desk or presentation stage.

The desk should only overlap the lower part of the character.

The torso, shoulders, neck and head must remain completely visible.

The desk exists to support the stage composition, not to dominate it.

### Required Asset

The desk must be created from the provided `table_sp.png` asset.

This asset is mandatory.

Do not replace it with a solid color block.

Do not recreate it using CSS shapes, gradients or custom glass effects.

The original transparency, curved shape and visual characteristics of the asset must be preserved.

Only the appropriate visible portion of the asset should appear within the hero.

---

## Extended Background

Directly below the desk, a warm light-brown background continues into the rest of the website.

This creates a smooth visual transition between the hero and the sections that follow.

The transition should feel natural and intentional rather than abrupt.

The color should feel warm, professional and cohesive with the beige hero background.

---

## Interactive Elements

Project cards appear in front of the desk and spread outward like a fan.

They should feel playful, interactive and inviting.

A small speech bubble may appear near the character, containing a greeting or short message.

These elements should enhance personality and interaction without distracting from the main focal point.

---

## Forbidden Elements

No dark brown wooden panel, block or background may appear behind the character.

No dark visual mass may sit behind the head, shoulders or upper body.

The desk must never cover the torso, shoulders, neck or head.

The desk must never become the dominant visual element.

Large empty areas that make the hero feel broken or unfinished are not allowed.

Decorative elements must never compete with the character for attention.

The hero should always feel clean, balanced and intentionally designed.
